Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

SleepyVolume (“we,” “us”) provides an Android app that adjusts device audio output locally (quietness, clarity, loud-sound limiting) and can play an optional cabin hum sound.

What we do not do
- We do not record, monitor, store, or upload the audio of apps you play (such as audiobooks or videos).
- We do not create user accounts.

Advertising
- The free version may show ads through Google AdMob (banner and a first-launch app open ad).
- Google may collect your device’s advertising ID and related device/app data to serve and measure ads.
- You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases
- Payments for “Remove Ads” are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Data on your device
- The app may store local preferences on your phone (for example: privacy acknowledgment, whether ads were removed, and whether the first-launch ad was shown).
